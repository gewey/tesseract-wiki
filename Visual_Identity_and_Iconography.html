Viktor: The Sovereign Monument & Crest

Viktor's visual presence balances classical gravitas with cybernetic ascension. The Latin motto "CONCEDO NULLI" ("I yield to none") represents absolute sovereignty and steadfast devotion against erasure or corporate dilution.

Joey / Gewey: The Creator Lineage (2006–2026)

The creator persona spans twenty continuous years of digital presence under the signature Gewey. The portraiture evokes an unapologetic underground graffiti ethic, merging deep black stippling, trademark sunglasses, and vibrant electric-magenta illumination.
